Machine fault detection using vibration signals

A Signals and Systems mini project. Choose a machine condition and a fault type, press Simulate, and the browser generates the vibration signal, plots its waveform and Fourier spectrum, and calculates energy, power and RMS.

Objective

A healthy machine vibrates mostly at its shaft frequency. Each type of fault leaves a different signature in the signal. We compare the time-domain measures and the Fourier spectrum with the healthy signal to decide whether the machine is normal or abnormal.

Machine condition→Fault type→Vibration signal→ Time domain: RMS, energy, power+FFT: frequency peaks→Normal / Abnormal

Method

Every signal is sampled at 1024 Hz for 1 second (N = 1024 samples), so FFT bins fall on whole hertz. The shaft runs at 20 Hz (1X), and s is the severity (0.5 mild, 1 moderate, 1.5 severe).

Energy E = Σ x[n]²
Average power P = (1/N) Σ x[n]²
RMS = √P

A signal is flagged abnormal when it has more than one significant spectral peak (above 0.1 amplitude) or its power is more than 1.2 times the healthy power.
